# Privacy Policy

Effective [MERGE DATE]

This policy explains what Road to Next Rating ("we," "us") collects, why, who helps us run the Service, and the choices you have. Questions: support@roadtonextrating.com.

## The short version
- We collect what the app needs to diagnose your game, build your plan, and track your practice.
- We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use ad or analytics trackers.
- You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings.

## What we collect

**Your assessment.** Your answers, your diagnosis, and your plan. These are saved to our database as soon as you finish the assessment, before you create an account, so we can show you your plan and keep it if you sign up. Your browser also keeps a copy and an ID so it can find your result again.

**Your account.** Your email address, a password (stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider), your email reminder settings, and when you agreed to these terms.

**Your practice.** The drill results you log and when you logged them, plus your re-test answers and results.

**Payments.** If you buy a paid plan, Stripe collects your payment details. We never see your full card number. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your plan type (monthly or yearly), its status, and your renewal date.

**Technical data.** Cookies that keep you signed in, browser storage that keeps your result on your device, and standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) that our hosting providers keep for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

## How we use it
- To run the Service: score your assessment, build your plan, record your sessions, and show your progress.
- To send emails you need: sign-up confirmation, password resets, session reminders, your re-test reminder, and billing emails.
- To process payments and manage your subscription.
- To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems, and answer support requests.
- To understand, in aggregate, how the Service is used (for example, which skills come up most often) so we can improve it.

## Who we share it with
We share data only with providers that help us run the Service, and only what each one needs:
- **Supabase**: database and sign-in
- **Vercel**: website hosting
- **Stripe**: payments and billing emails
- **Resend**: sending emails
- **ImprovMX**: forwarding email sent to our support address

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business (with notice to you). We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.

## Emails
- Account emails (confirmation, password reset) are always sent because the Service needs them.
- Session reminders are on by default while your plan is active, and they stop after your re-test date. We send one re-test reminder when your re-test opens. You can turn either off in Settings, and every reminder has an unsubscribe link that works without signing in.
- Billing emails (receipts and renewal notices) come from Stripe.

## How long we keep it
- **Account data** (answers, diagnoses, plans, practice logs): until you delete your account.
- **Results never saved to an account**: deleted after 30 days.
- **Sign-ups never confirmed**: deleted after 30 days.
- **Payment records**: kept by Stripe and by us as long as tax and accounting rules require, even after you delete your account.
- **Server logs and backups**: kept by our providers for a limited time, then deleted.

## Deleting your account
Go to Settings and choose Delete account. This deletes your account, answers, diagnoses, plans, and practice logs, signs you out, and clears your result from that browser. It also cancels any paid plan right away (see our Terms). Payment records stay with Stripe as described above, and copies in backups expire on their normal schedule.

You can also email us to access, correct, or delete your information. We'll respond within 45 days.

## Children
The Service is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

## Security
We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and database access rules that keep each player's data private to them. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## Where your data is stored
Your data is stored and processed in the United States [CONFIRM: Supabase and Vercel regions].

## Do Not Track
We don't track you across other websites, so we don't change anything in response to Do Not Track signals.

## Changes to this policy
If we make a material change, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
