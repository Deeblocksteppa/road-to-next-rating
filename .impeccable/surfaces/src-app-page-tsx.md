# Marketing site (`/`)

**Scope:** the public storefront at `/`. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The anonymous
assessment funnel at `/start` and the signed-in app are out of scope.

**Audience & job:** a cold 3.0–4.0 pickleball player arriving from a TikTok or
Instagram link who has never heard of this product. They believe their game has
many scattered weaknesses. Success is one action: starting the free assessment.

**Action:** `Find your bottleneck — free` → `/start`. Repeated in the hero, the
free pricing card, and the closing section. No secondary conversion goal.

**Proof:** real app screenshots only. There are no customers, testimonials, or
usage data (PRODUCT.md), so credibility comes from the specificity of the
diagnosis itself — the two-axis mechanism section carries the whole argument.
Never add counts, logos, or quotes.

**Constraints:** the app's design system unchanged — no shadows, no gradients in
UI chrome, no photography, illustration, mascots, or gamification. Accent
appears once per viewport. Register targets linear.app / vercel.com.

**Direction:** the page runs the app's own reveal choreography as its spine —
flat and quiet, one idea per section, the accent only where something is
actually being pointed at. Memorable moment: the hero verdict screenshot under a
single ambient optic field, the one place on the page light is used at all.

**Deliberate exception:** the hero glow is a radial optic field, the one
sanctioned departure from DESIGN.md's Kill-the-Gradient rule, which governs UI
chrome. Scoped to the hero only.

**Unresolved:** `plan.png` is the one uncaptured shot; the Plan section runs on
the session capture instead. `progress.png` and `retest-delta.png` come from
different demo runs and their figures do not reconcile — captioned as separate
examples for now, but they should be recaptured from one fixture. `/privacy` and
`/terms` ship as factual summaries, not reviewed legal documents.
